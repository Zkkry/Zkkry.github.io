About Me

My Background

Im Muhammad Zikry bin Khairul Lizam, an Industrial Electronics Automation undergraduate at Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA), where I’ve been studying since September 2024 and currently hold a CGPA of 3.44. I’m from Merlimau, Jasin, Melaka.

My path started in IT. I completed a Diploma of Technology in Database Management System and Web Application at Kolej Vokasional Datuk Seri Abu Zahar Isnin (CGPA 3.78), along with my SVM and SKM Level 3 in Application Development. During that time I did an internship as an IT Technician at Majlis Perbandaran Jasin. There I handled hardware maintenance, troubleshot server and network issues, prepared ISO audit documentation, and installed internet cabling for a new office. I then worked in quality control at Xinyi Glass Holdings, where I inspected products and verified test results against company standards. At UMPSA, I moved into industrial automation. I work with PLC programming and HMI configuration using Omron and Wecon systems, the Omron MX2 VFD, and the Omron TM5 collaborative robot. I’m also certified in Omron COBOT (2025). On the software side, I use CX-Programmer, GT Designer3, EPLAN, AutoCAD and Codesys. My projects include two IoT systems for KV Pertanian Chenor under the SULAM Program (a portable chicks counterweight and a water quality monitoring system) and a Teaching Factory automotive absorber assembly production line. Because of my mix of IT, web development (HTML, CSS, PHP, MySQL) and automation skills, I’m comfortable working across both hardware and software.
